# Family Tree – Privacy Policy

Developer: Juraij Labs (MUHAMMED JURAIJ)
Contact: juraijlabs@gmail.com
Effective date: 25 November 2026

This policy explains how the Family Tree app for Android ("the app") handles your information.

## Summary
Family Tree works completely offline. Everything you enter stays on your phone. We do not collect, upload, sell or share any of your data, and the app has no ads, no analytics and no account sign-up.

## Information you enter
You can add family members and details about them, such as names, nicknames, house names, dates of birth, marriage and death, relationships, phone numbers, photos and notes. All of this is stored only in the app's storage on your own device. It is never sent to us or to anyone else, and we have no way to see it.

## Photos
When you add a photo to a person, you choose it with Android's photo picker. The app only gets access to the photos you pick, and a copy is kept inside the app on your device.

## Contacts
You can pick a phone number from your phone's contacts when adding a person. The app only receives the single number you choose. It does not read or store your contact list.

## Fingerprint lock
If you turn on the app lock, fingerprint or face checks are done by Android itself. The app never receives or stores your fingerprint or face data.

## Calls, messages and WhatsApp
Buttons to call, send an SMS or open WhatsApp simply open those apps on your phone. Nothing is sent by Family Tree itself.

## Backups, PDFs and sharing
Backups and exported files (such as PDF family charts or family book files) are saved on your device. They only leave your phone if you choose to share them, for example through WhatsApp or email. When you share a copy, the app lets you hide living people's photos and phone numbers.

## Data we collect
None. The app does not collect personal or usage data, does not use advertising or analytics services, and does not share data with third parties.

## Deleting your data
You can delete any person from inside the app (deleted items go to the recycle bin until you empty it). To delete everything, use Android Settings → Apps → Family Tree → Storage → Clear data, or uninstall the app. Backup files you saved or shared must be deleted separately.

## Children
The app is not directed at children under 13.

## Changes to this policy
If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
